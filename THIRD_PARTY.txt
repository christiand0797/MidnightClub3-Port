These run on the player's PC to build HD textures, PBR maps and HD movies from the
player's own disc. Each keeps its own license; full texts are at the links.

  https://www.python.org/  (license: enhance\python\LICENSE.txt)
NumPy - BSD-3-Clause - https://numpy.org/
SciPy - BSD-3-Clause - https://scipy.org/
Pillow - MIT-CMU (HPND) - https://python-pillow.org/
ONNX Runtime (DirectML build) - MIT - https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
  includes Microsoft DirectML, redistributable under its license - https://github.com/microsoft/DirectML
Their bundled dependencies (protobuf, flatbuffers, sympy, mpmath, packaging, coloredlogs, humanfriendly)
  keep their licenses in each package's .dist-info folder.
Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan (realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan.exe) - BSD-3-Clause, Xintao Wang
  https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN-ncnn-vulkan ; built on Tencent ncnn (BSD-3-Clause)
  https://github.com/Tencent/ncnn ; vcomp140.dll is the Microsoft OpenMP runtime (Visual C++ redistributable)
4x-UltraSharp (Kim2091) - CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 - https://openmodeldb.info/models/4x-UltraSharp

  (the unmodified network; its source is at that address)
PBRify_Remix (Kim2091) - CC0 1.0 - https://github.com/Kim2091/PBRify_Remix
  (the three networks exported to ONNX by tools/pbr/generate.py --export-onnx)
